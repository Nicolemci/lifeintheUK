# App Store screenshots (no Watch)

Upload under **App Previews and Screenshots**. Up to 10 screenshots per device size. App Previews (video) are optional — not included here.

## Required

### iPhone with Dynamic Island (medium display)
Folder: `iphone-dynamic-island/` · **1206 × 2622** portrait

Drag all 8 PNGs into this tab.

Folder: `ipad-13/` · **2064 × 2752** portrait

Drag all 8 PNGs into this tab (required if the app supports iPad).
